# Brandbook v2 — changes
Date: 2026-11-04 · Baseline: docs/brand-review.html; docs/tankhor-technical-spec.md §2.1, §12 · New: docs/brand/brandbook.html

## Summary
- The brown light/dark palette is numerically unchanged; the meaningful-control-boundary token is named `borderControl` instead of `borderStrong`.
- The new guide again treats brown and jade as unapproved alternatives, conflicting with the recorded Q02 brown decision; jade itself was already proposed in the brand review.
- Three explicit tonal scales and four surface/gradient recipes are added; the scales are exploratory, not replacements for semantic tokens.
- Typography narrows size ranges to specific large/small-screen values, changes desktop-title and section-title line heights, and replaces Tahoma fallback with Arial; Vazirmatn, weights 400/500/600 and SIL OFL 1.1 stay the same.
- Logo work is assigned to the owner, all nine assistant concepts are declared inactive, and the earlier direction-selection brief is no longer carried forward; the in-house Lottie/SVG and curtain/hand deliverable needs confirmation against Q04.
- Icon guidance adds a 24 px canvas and 1.5–2 px stroke; the new radius set omits 24 px, and the 48 px primary-button height is not restated.
- Motion ranges become proposed point values: 180 ms control feedback and 500 ms curtain/state continuity; reduced-motion and real-readiness rules remain.
- The guide adds interactive teaching tools, embedded font/licence and token/copy handoff data, and a catalogue of ten application templates in both palettes; linked template/PDF files are not supplied here.
- New naming notes introduce the unreserved domain candidates `Tankhor.io` and `Tankhoor.com` and an unsourced prior-use report; the brand and experience names themselves do not change.
- Copy still uses the common noun «تن‌خور», keeps Q30 open, and qualifies some forbidden claims as “without evidence”; these conflict with the current spec/decision record and need owner review, not automatic adoption.
- The guide condenses the earlier strategy/research and decision-register coverage; omitted requirements, including system-theme support, are not treated as cancelled.
- Decisions affected: Q02 yes · Q03 yes · Q04 yes
